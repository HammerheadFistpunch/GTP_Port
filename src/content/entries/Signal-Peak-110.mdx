I recent update to Signal Peak, my radio propagation mapping and modeling tool. Fixed a few bugs, but the biggest changes are:

1. No longer region locked to Utah. Now available for the Continental US
2. Updated output heatmaps that are more clear in terms of the signal gradient quality
3. an inverse overlay that shows where signal ISN'T likely on the map based on the most available data.
