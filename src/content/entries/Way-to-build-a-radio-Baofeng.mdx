Boafeng radios are such a great value in amateur radio, but they definitely prove the axiom that you get what you pay for.  

![UV17R](https://www.baofengradio.com/cdn/shop/files/UV-17R-5W-Dual-Band-Radio-Baofeng-6635.jpg)

I've like my UV-17R, its more weather resistant, it has a clearer display and its got a usb-c charged battery. What mine also has was a rattle. I kinda assumed it was something in the light module on the bottom. Everything work, no complaints, but I decided to finally pull it apart to see what was up.  Well, it wasn't the light.

![uv17r1](https://media.angrysquirrel.org/immich/0840edd7-7978-4690-9239-8e5a684822e9/ohaOMHpsZaY_oQoXmh8U5lJuteY/v1/web)

![uv17r2](https://media.angrysquirrel.org/immich/006f362e-7ce6-42c7-93fa-d9020d789a3a/0fJjyLHIBC9t-63vEmZp0qSEjm0/v1/web)

![screwed](https://media.angrysquirrel.org/immich/20f69a77-fac9-40b3-a6b4-cd0bb86c0fa4/dyuZ3a2_8xbA-R3WfBTht7OL-qA/v1/web)

Well, I found the rattle.  Thats not supposed to be in there.  I looked to see what came unscrewed and nothing. It's just an extra screw in there. Honestly Im amazing it hasn't caused a short considering it was just rolling around
