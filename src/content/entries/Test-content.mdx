The bottoms of our shoes are terrible. I hate to draw a mental picture but think of the places those soles have tread, from dirty streets and sticky floors to the dirt and grime of the job site. Would you want them on the carpets in your home?  Me either, so why would we allow them on our carpets in our cars?

When I bought my last car the previous owner didn’t seem to care one way or another and as a result there is a large hole in the carpets – right about where high heels rested – all the way to sheet metal.  I told myself that if I couldn’t stop that problem there I would stop it from happening elsewhere.

All new cars these days, with some exception, come with floor mats at some basic level but many can leave a lot to be desired for quality and protection and if you want more comfort or protection or if you are replacing worn out or missing mats you owe it to yourself to consider floor mats.

The purpose of this buyers guide is to give you a broad scope of some of your options so you can choose the right level of comfort and performance for your budget

If you boil it down there are 3 basic types of mats or liners to look at.

Basic – inexpensive replacements for basic protection

Premium – An upgrade in quality of materials and improvements in feel and durability

All-weather – designed with protection first and foremost, including mud, snow sand and other extreme elements.

BASIC

Basic mats aren’t a bad choice for any vehicle, as I said before feet and shoes are gross, why put a lot of money into something literally trodden under foot?  If you are looking for floor mats you are in one of 2 boats, you need more protection or you need SOME protection for lost mats, these mats are for the second choice.  If you’ve got an old truck, or wheeling rig that you will get messy but don’t want to wear out the difficult to repair factory carpet you won’t go wrong here.  These are typically one size fits all and are made from nylon loop carpet or rubber.  Even though they are considered a basic mat, you can often find quality items for less than you would spend at your local parts store online like the pro Z line that are not only more flexible and durable than cheap store brands but aren’t much different in price.  [https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/75A6424A0A0.aspx](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/75A6424A0A0.aspx)

Something to consider if you are looking in this category is what kind of environment you will be subjecting the mats to. If wet and muddy or in winter conditions consider a rubber mat with deep grooves to retain the mess.  A mat like the Michelin rubber mat kit suits this need well.

If you are in a hot dry location like I am, consider a thin pile nylon loop carpet like the ProZ big rig for trucks and SUV’s ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/proz-bigrig-carpet-floor-mats](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/proz-bigrig-carpet-floor-mats)) or the lloyds classic loop ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A6473A0A0.aspx](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A6473A0A0.aspx)) that will capture and retain fine dirt and sand easily but be simply to clean at the car wash.

PREMIUM

As the name implies, these mats are either replacement mats for premium vehicles or increased protection without sacrificing a premium look and feel in premium vehicles.

Lloyds makes a premium custom fit deep pile carpet mat that is a great solution if you drive barefoot or just like the feel of a deep pile carpet.  ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A4427A0A0.aspx](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A4427A0A0.aspx))

Or custom fit embroidered mats to personalize the interior of your vehicle ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A4395A0A0.aspx](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/78A4395A0A0.aspx))

When looking in this category be careful not to just shop for the most expensive assuming the most luxurious, there are bargains here to be found too like the ProZ Premium custom fit which are a quality nylon pile and custom fit for substantially less

For 3 pedal heel-toe addicts that refuse to give up a carpet feel the Lund Catch-all mats provide a premium mat with reinforcement for your fancy footwork.  ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/lund-catch-all-floor-mats](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/lund-catch-all-floor-mats))

All-Weather

If you are shopping here it’s because, like me, you live in a place with nasty winters and you’d like to stop soaking your carpets with slush and crud, or maybe you just really would rather not have a spilled coffee spoil your brand new carpets, either way the goal here is mess containment.

This category ranges the spectrum from basic to premium.  The Gold standard in this are for years have been liners like the Weathertech Digifit ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/weathertech-digitalfit-floor-liners](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/weathertech-digitalfit-floor-liners)) and Husky liners ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/husky-liners-x-act-contour-floor-liners](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/husky-liners-x-act-contour-floor-liners)) which are premium quality protection that not only perfectly contours to your specific make and model (where available) but class up the place a little in the process.  There is an old saying that the clean engine bay gets a little more of the white glove treatment in the mechanics bay, likewise having premium protection visible when you open the door tells everyone you love your vehicle and are serious about keeping it nice. Hopefully this encourages your guests to do the same but even if they don’t these premium all-weather liners can take it.  I’ve had weathertech Digifit and Husky Liners in my personal vehicles in the past and I can attest to their durability and quality.

These mats, typically called floor liners, are hard and durable but don’t provide the look or feel or carpet, in those cases looking at something like the Aries StyleGuard XD ([https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/aries-styleguard-xd-floor-liners](https://www.autoanything.com/floor-mats/aries-styleguard-xd-floor-liners)) or Lund Catch-it Carpet mats provide excellent protection with a more pleasing feel.

Whatever mat you choose, make sure you also consider anchoring and fit to ensure safety.
